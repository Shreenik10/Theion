# Sample Conversation with Saylor Lee

## Initial Contact
**Nurse:** Hello, I'm the nurse who will be doing your initial assessment today. How are you doing?  
**Saylor:** I'm doing well, thank you. How are you?

**Nurse:** What brings you in today?  
**Saylor:** Well, I moved from Ottawa to Montreal about six months ago. I'm new to this clinic and here for an initial check‑up.

**Nurse:** Before we begin, I need your consent to proceed with the assessment.  
**Saylor:** Yes, I consent.

## Medical History and Medications
**Nurse:** Let's start with your medical history. Do you have any ongoing medical conditions?  
**Saylor:** I was diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes about 5 years ago. I've also experienced anxiety on and off with major life events. I was diagnosed and started treatment for anxiety with my healthcare practitioner about 2 years ago. The medication has helped a lot.

**Nurse:** What medications are you currently taking?  
**Saylor:** I'm currently taking Metformin 500mg twice a day for diabetes and Cipralex 10mg daily - I've been on that for anxiety for about 2 years now.

**Nurse:** Do you take any over-the-counter medications?  
**Saylor:** I take Tylenol occasionally for headaches - about once a month. When I do take it, I take 650mg, which is two tablets.

**Nurse:** How about any supplements or herbal products?  
**Saylor:** I don't take any herbal supplements or natural products.

## Allergies
**Nurse:** Do you have any allergies?  
**Saylor:** I'm allergic to peanuts.

**Nurse:** Can you tell me what happens when you have an allergic reaction?  
**Saylor:** My last reaction was a few years ago - my face got swollen and I had difficulty breathing.

**Nurse:** Do you carry an epipen?  
**Saylor:** I don't carry an epi-pen, but I know how to use one - 'Orange to the thigh and blue to the sky.'

**Nurse:** Any medication allergies?  
**Saylor:** I don't have any allergies to medications.

**Nurse:** Any environmental allergies?  
**Saylor:** I don't have any environmental allergies.

**Nurse:** Let's do a review of your body systems. How's your head, eyes, ears, nose, and throat?

**Nurse:** How about your breathing?

**Nurse:** Any heart problems?

**Nurse:** Any circulation problems?

**Nurse:** Any muscle or joint problems?  
**Saylor:** I don't have any musculoskeletal issues.

**Nurse:** Any neurological issues?  
**Saylor:** I don't have any neurological issues.

**Saylor:** I don't have any mobility issues.

**Saylor:** I don't need any assistive devices.

## Lifestyle and Activities
**Nurse:** What physical activities do you do?  
**Saylor:** My main physical activities are walking and golf.

**Nurse:** Can you tell me more about your walking routine?  
**Saylor:** I used to walk for about 30 minutes every evening, but I haven't been able to walk as much since moving into town. I would very much like to get back to my daily walks.

**Nurse:** How's your sleep?  
**Saylor:** I usually get about 6 hours of sleep per night. I don't wake up during the night, but I do have a hard time falling asleep because I tend to make mental to-do lists.

## Substance Use
**Nurse:** Do you smoke or use any tobacco products?  
**Saylor:** I currently vape. I don't have any other history of smoking.

**Nurse:** Can you tell me more about your vaping?  
**Saylor:** I go through about one cartridge per day, and I've been vaping for 5 years now. The cartridges contain nicotine. It used to help me with stress.

**Nurse:** How about alcohol use?  
**Saylor:** I usually have one beer or glass of wine when I get home from work, sometimes two on weekends.

**Nurse:** Any recreational drug use?  
**Saylor:** I've never used any substances.

## Diabetes Management and Diet
**Nurse:** Let's talk about your diabetes management. How's that going?  
**Saylor:** I have diabetes, but it's well controlled.

**Nurse:** Have you had any unintentional weight loss, fever, chills, or night sweats?  
**Saylor:** No, I haven't had any unintentional weight loss, fever, chills, or night sweats.

**Nurse:** Tell me about your diet.  
**Saylor:** I made drastic changes to my diet when I was diagnosed with diabetes 5 years ago. I worked with a diabetes team who helped me revamp my lifestyle - I really didn't want to have to start insulin. My partner started following a vegetarian diet, so I'm doing that as well now.

**Nurse:** Can you give me an example of what you eat in a typical day?  
**Saylor:** For breakfast, I had oatmeal with fresh berries. Lunch was lentil soup with a mixed green salad. Dinner was a vegetable stir-fry with tofu. I snack on nuts and fruit throughout the day.
